Add these in Cloudflare Pages → Settings → Variables and Secrets, then deploy again. Store the secret privately; never paste it into storefront code.

Direct UPI

Customers can launch their UPI app with the amount and order number pre-filled.

Set your UPI ID in Website & policies → Contact & store operations.
Direct UPI intent is not payment verification. Confirm receipt in your bank/UPI app before marking an order paid. Razorpay provides automated verification.
