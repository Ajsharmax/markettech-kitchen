A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Built from a service-first point of view.

I’ve spent years working in customer-facing roles, including banking and relationship management. That experience taught me that a good customer experience is not just about selling something — it is about listening, communicating clearly and being there when help is needed.

I’m bringing that same mindset to MarketTech. The goal is to build a store where the products are practical, the information is easy to understand and customers feel comfortable reaching out.

MarketTech is growing step by step, with a focus on kitchen and household products that can earn a useful place in everyday homes.
